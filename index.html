🎙️ VOICE GLOW KIDS ACADEMY

Young School Reporter

Let's interview our school principal!

Lights, microphone, confidence!

Prepare your questions, practice your voice, and become a confident young presenter.

⭐ Lesson Steps

💬 Suggested Questions

  1. What inspired you to become a school principal?
  2. What is your favorite thing about our school?
  3. What makes a good student?
  4. How do you encourage students to achieve their dreams?
  5. What new activities would you like to introduce at school?
  6. What advice would you give to students who feel shy?
  7. What is your happiest memory as a principal?
  8. What message would you like to share with all students?

🎧 Your Recording

Practice your presenter voice and record your introduction or interview.

Your recording stays on this device until you choose to share it.

💡 Little Reporter Tips

  • Speak clearly and smile.
  • Use a friendly tone.
  • Ask one question at a time.
  • Listen carefully to the answer.
  • Be confident and respectful!
